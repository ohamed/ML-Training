# Task: Prepare an "AI Strategy at ITE" Document

Act as an AI strategy consultant with expertise in enterprise AI adoption, research organizations, and technology management.

Prepare a professional document titled **"AI Strategy at ITE"**, intended for the Head of the Institute of Technology and Engineering (ITE), Forschungszentrum Jülich.

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to provide a concise strategic overview of the potential adoption of AI chatbots at ITE and support the Head of ITE in approving their acquisition and use across different ITE departments.

The document should explain why AI chatbots are relevant to ITE, how they could support different departments, what benefits they offer, and what organizational and financial considerations should guide their adoption.

The intended outcome is to obtain management approval to acquire team-based AI chatbot subscriptions for professional and research-related use within ITE.

## 2. Source Material

I have uploaded a PowerPoint presentation titled "AI Cost Estimation_ITE.pptx".

You must carefully review the entire presentation before writing.

Use the presentation as the primary source for:
- AI chatbot applications and use cases.
- Comparison of available AI providers.
- Team subscription pricing.
- Estimated acquisition costs.
- Data protection considerations.

Transform the presentation content into a coherent management-oriented document rather than simply summarizing individual slides.

You may consult official vendor websites to verify current subscription prices, features, and licensing conditions. Clearly distinguish verified information from estimates in the presentation.

## 3. Main Focus: Team-Based AI Chatbot Subscriptions

This is the most important part of the document.

Focus specifically on acquiring team-based subscriptions rather than individual personal subscriptions.

Prioritize the following solutions:

**A. Standard Team Plans**

Give particular attention to Standard Team subscriptions from:

- Anthropic Claude Team Standard: approximately €20 per user per month (the presentation lists €18/month plus VAT).
- OpenAI ChatGPT Business Standard: approximately €26 per user per month.
- Google AI team offering: include the relevant team subscription and pricing from the presentation.
- Perplexity Enterprise/Team offering: include only if relevant to the comparison and supported by the presentation.

Explain why team subscriptions are more appropriate for ITE than individual subscriptions, particularly regarding:
- Shared team workspaces and collaboration.
- Centralized billing and subscription management.
- Business data protection.
- Organizational use across different departments.
- Access to advanced AI capabilities for research, engineering, programming, and documentation.

The financial assessment should focus on the Standard Team tier, which generally costs around €20–30 per user per month, depending on the provider and applicable VAT.

**B. Langdock**

Include Langdock as an additional solution in the comparison.

Use the following pricing information:

- Langdock Team subscription: €31.20 per user per month, plus VAT.
- Monthly subscription model.

Explain Langdock's potential relevance as an AI platform for organizational use, particularly its ability to provide access to different AI models through a centralized environment.

Assess its potential advantages for ITE, including centralized administration, model flexibility, and organizational control.

Clearly distinguish Langdock from individual chatbot subscriptions. Do not assume that its features or contractual conditions are identical to those of Claude, ChatGPT, or other providers. Verify relevant details using official Langdock documentation where necessary.

**C. Financial Estimation**

Provide a concise financial assessment based primarily on Standard Team subscriptions and Langdock.

Use the following illustrative assumptions:

- Approximately 60–65 potential users across ITE.
- Monthly subscriptions.
- Standard Team subscriptions costing approximately €20–30 per user per month.
- Langdock Team subscriptions costing €31.20 per user per month, plus VAT.

Include an indicative annual cost calculation for 60 and 65 users.

Show the difference between:
- Standard Team subscriptions at approximately €20 per user per month.
- Standard Team subscriptions at approximately €25 per user per month.
- Langdock Team subscriptions at €31.20 per user per month, excluding VAT.

Clearly identify these calculations as budget estimates, not confirmed procurement quotations.

Do not assume that all employees must receive the same subscription. Mention the possibility of allocating licenses according to departmental needs and actual usage.

## 4. Strategic Rationale and Potential Applications

Explain how AI chatbots can support ITE's multidisciplinary research and engineering activities.

Include relevant use cases across different departments and professional functions, such as:

- Scientific research and literature reviews.
- Summarizing scientific papers and technical documents.
- Software development and programming assistance.
- Data analysis and interpretation.
- Engineering and technical problem-solving.
- Technical documentation and report preparation.
- Administrative tasks and internal communication.
- Knowledge retrieval and information management.

Prioritize examples relevant to ITE.

## 5. Expected Benefits for ITE

Summarize the expected benefits:

- Increased productivity and reduced time spent on routine tasks.
- Improved support for scientific research and engineering activities.
- Faster access to information and technical knowledge.
- Assistance with programming, documentation, and data analysis.
- Improved collaboration and knowledge sharing.
- Access to different AI models and capabilities, depending on the selected solution.

Avoid unsupported quantitative claims about productivity improvements.

## 6. Data Protection and Implementation Considerations

Briefly address:
- Business and team subscription data protection policies.
- Confidentiality and information security.
- GDPR and applicable institutional requirements.
- Appropriate use of cloud-based AI services.
- Internal guidelines and acceptable-use policies.
- User training and awareness.
- Licensing administration and budget monitoring.

Emphasize that ITE should select solutions based on its institutional security and procurement requirements.

Do not assume that a paid team subscription automatically satisfies all Forschungszentrum Jülich requirements.

## 7. Recommendation and Proposed Next Steps

Conclude with a clear recommendation for management consideration.

Propose:
1. Approval in principle for acquiring team-based AI chatbot subscriptions.
2. Selection of suitable providers, prioritizing Standard Team plans and evaluating Langdock as an additional option.
3. Allocation of licenses to interested employees across relevant ITE departments.
4. An initial implementation or pilot phase.
5. Evaluation of user feedback, productivity, practical benefits, and costs.
6. Review of the results before considering broader deployment.

Clearly state the management approval being requested.

Produce a polished, management-ready document titled:

**AI Strategy at ITE**
