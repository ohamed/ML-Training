You are an expert researcher in Natural Language Processing, Information Extraction, Knowledge Graph Construction, and academic writing.

Your task is to write only the first paragraph of the Introduction for an ACL-style research paper describing our participation in the KnowledgeGraphEval 2026 Shared Task, Subtask 1: AdaptNER | Cross-Domain Named Entity Recognition.

The uploaded research papers are the primary source of information. Base the paragraph mainly on these papers. You may use the shared task description for background information and only introduce additional information if it is essential and well supported.

## Objective

Write a concise opening paragraph that introduces the problem of Named Entity Recognition (NER) and motivates its importance.

The paragraph should:

* Begin with the general concept of Named Entity Recognition.
* Explain why NER is a fundamental Information Extraction task.
* Briefly describe its role in downstream NLP applications, including Information Retrieval, Question Answering, Knowledge Graph Construction, and Relation Extraction.
* Explain why Arabic Named Entity Recognition remains challenging because of the linguistic characteristics of Arabic, domain variation, and the limited availability of annotated datasets across different domains.
* Introduce the challenge of cross-domain NER, emphasizing that models trained on one domain often experience substantial performance degradation when applied to unseen domains.
* End by motivating the need for robust domain-adaptive Arabic NER systems, naturally leading to the remainder of the introduction.

## Writing Requirements

* Write only one paragraph.
* The paragraph should be no longer than about 15 lines in the ACL two-column LaTeX format (approximately 150–200 words). No less than 150 words.
* Use simple, clear, and objective academic language.
* Use short sentences.
* Follow the writing style of ACL, EMNLP, or NAACL papers.
* Maintain a logical flow from general background to the specific problem.
* Do not describe our proposed system.
* Do not discuss experiments or results.
* Do not mention the paper organization.
* Avoid marketing language and unsupported claims.
* Avoid repeating ideas.

## Citations

Prefer citing the uploaded papers.
