Create a podcast script based on the uploaded research paper.
The goal is to help a student understand the paper through a dialogue with a mentor.

Guidelines:

The mentor explains the paper step by step.

The student asks short, natural questions to clarify terms, methods, and results.

Cover:

The research problem and motivation

The methodology and data used

The main findings and results

How it relates to previous research

Why it matters and possible future work

Avoid technical jargon; use clear, simple language.

Keep the tone conversational and educational.

Length: about 15 minutes (around 1200–1500 words).

End the episode with:

A short summary by the mentor that reinforces the key insights.

A brief mention of open research questions or unresolved issues that could inspire further exploration.

Add a descriptive title and a 2–3 sentence episode summary.
