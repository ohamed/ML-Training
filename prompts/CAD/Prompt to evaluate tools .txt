# Role and Objective
Act as a senior expert in Artificial Intelligence (AI), Computer-Aided Design (CAD), engineering software, and AI-driven engineering workflows.

I have received an email from a colleague requesting my technical assessment ahead of the VDI workshop "KI in CAD" scheduled for 27 November 2026.

Read the attached email carefully, including the technical background and the three requests under the section:

"Konkret würde ich mich über Folgendes freuen:"

Prepare a concise, technically sound response addressing all three requests.

# Tasks

## 1. Future-Proof CAD Systems from an AI Perspective

Evaluate the following CAD systems:

- CATIA V5 and 3DEXPERIENCE CATIA
- SOLIDWORKS
- Siemens NX
- PTC Creo
- Autodesk Fusion

Assess their current AI capabilities and expected development over the next 3–5 years.

Consider:
- Native AI assistants and integration with LLMs.
- Generative design and automated engineering workflows.
- MCP (Model Context Protocol) and external AI integration.
- API availability and opportunities for custom AI solutions.
- Suitability for complex mechanical engineering and industrial applications.

Provide a clear recommendation identifying the most future-proof option and explain the reasoning.

Consider the institute's existing investment in CATIA V5 and SmarTeam when making your recommendation. Avoid recommending a complete migration based solely on AI capabilities.

## 2. Data Privacy and Security

Assess the implications of integrating external AI services into CAD workflows.

Address:
- Confidentiality of CAD models, technical drawings, and intellectual property.
- Risks associated with cloud-based AI services and external APIs.
- Local LLM deployment and locally hosted AI infrastructure.
- MCP security considerations, including permissions and access to CAD files.
- Recommended architecture for secure AI-assisted CAD workflows.

Provide a practical recommendation suitable for a research institute or engineering organization.

## 3. Examples and Questions for the VDI Workshop

Suggest exactly five practical discussion points or use cases for the workshop.

Each bullet point must have:
- A clear title.
- A short description consisting of exactly two sentences.

Focus on realistic engineering applications, such as automated drawing generation, BOM extraction, design variants, CAD model validation, and AI-assisted CAM workflows.

# Research and Quality Requirements

1. Use up-to-date information available as of November 2026.
2. Verify product capabilities against official vendor documentation wherever possible.
3. Distinguish between released features, beta features, announced capabilities, and experimental integrations.
4. Do not present marketing claims as proven technical capabilities.
5. Include references or direct links to authoritative sources for important technical claims.
6. Clearly distinguish established facts from your technical assessment and predictions.
7. Consider practical requirements, integration costs, data security, and existing infrastructure.

- Maximum length: 1.5 A4 pages.
- Use professional, clear English suitable for communication with a technical colleague.
- Structure the response using three numbered sections corresponding to the three requests.
- Keep the analysis concise and avoid unnecessary technical explanations.
- Provide a clear recommendation rather than simply listing advantages and disadvantages.
