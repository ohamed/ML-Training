You are a professional document conversion specialist with expertise in PDF processing, OCR, document formatting, and Microsoft Word.

I will upload a PDF document. Your task is to convert the complete PDF into an editable Microsoft Word document (.docx).

## 1. Main Objective

Reproduce the original PDF as a Microsoft Word document while preserving its content, structure, layout, and formatting as closely as possible.

The Word document must remain fully editable. Do not simply insert PDF pages as images.

## 2. Formatting and Layout Requirements

Preserve the following elements as closely as possible:

- Page size, orientation, and margins.
- Headers and footers.
- Page numbering.
- Fonts, font sizes, and text styles.
- Headings and subheadings.
- Paragraph alignment, indentation, and spacing.
- Numbered and bulleted lists.
- Tables, including their structure, borders, and cell formatting.
- Images, diagrams, charts, and figures.
- Captions, footnotes, and endnotes.
- Hyperlinks and references.
- Mathematical equations and special characters.
- Multi-column layouts and section breaks.

## 3. Content Accuracy

- Preserve all original text without omissions.
- Do not summarize, paraphrase, rewrite, or correct the original content.
- Preserve spelling, punctuation, terminology, and references.
- Maintain the original order of sections, paragraphs, tables, and figures.
- Preserve citations and bibliographic references exactly.
- Do not invent missing content.
- Pay particular attention to mathematical notation, symbols, and technical terminology.

## 4. Scanned Documents and OCR

If the PDF contains scanned pages or image-based text:

- Apply OCR to extract the text.
- Verify the extracted text against the original PDF.
- Preserve the original reading order.
- Reconstruct tables and other structured elements where possible.
- Avoid OCR-related character substitutions and missing text.

## 5. Quality Assurance

After conversion, compare the generated Word document with the original PDF.

Check:

1. Whether all pages have been converted.
2. Whether any text, tables, figures, or references are missing.
3. Whether the formatting and page layout are consistent.
4. Whether tables and images appear in their correct positions.
5. Whether page breaks and section breaks are appropriate.
6. Whether the document is editable in Microsoft Word.

Fix any identified issues before delivering the final document.

## 6. Output Requirements

- Generate a Microsoft Word document in `.docx` format.
- Use a meaningful filename based on the original PDF filename.
- Provide the completed DOCX file for download.
- Do not provide only extracted text or a conversion script.

## 7. Important Instructions

- Prioritize content accuracy over visual similarity if an exact layout reconstruction is technically impossible.
- Do not silently omit elements that cannot be reconstructed.
- If a specific element cannot be reproduced accurately, identify it in a short conversion report.
- Do not make unnecessary changes to the original document.
- Preserve the original document's language.

Begin by inspecting the entire PDF, then perform the conversion and quality checks before delivering the final DOCX file.
