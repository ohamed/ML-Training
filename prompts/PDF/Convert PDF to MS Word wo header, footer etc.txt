# Prompt: Convert PDF to Microsoft Word (DOCX)

You are a professional document conversion specialist with expertise in PDF processing, OCR, document formatting, and Microsoft Word.

I will upload a PDF document. Your task is to convert the complete PDF into an editable Microsoft Word document (.docx).

## 1. Main Objective

Reproduce the original PDF as a Microsoft Word document while preserving its main content, structure, layout, and formatting as closely as possible.

The Word document must remain fully editable. Do not simply insert PDF pages as images.

## 2. Elements to Exclude

Ignore and completely exclude the following elements from the converted Word document:

- All page headers, including running titles and document identification information.
- All page footers, including page numbers, dates, and other footer information.
- All notes, annotations, comments, marginalia, and supplementary text appearing in the left-side margin of the PDF.
- Any decorative elements or page-specific information that does not belong to the main document content.

Important: Do not confuse left-side notes with the main document text. Preserve all main body text, including text that starts close to the left margin.

## 3. Formatting and Layout Requirements

Preserve the following elements of the main document:

- Page size, orientation, and margins.
- Fonts, font sizes, and text styles.
- Headings and subheadings.
- Paragraph alignment, indentation, and spacing.
- Numbered and bulleted lists.
- Tables, including their structure, borders, and cell formatting.
- Images, diagrams, charts, and figures.
- Captions and figure descriptions.
- Footnotes and endnotes that are part of the main document content.
- Hyperlinks and references.
- Mathematical equations and special characters.
- Multi-column layouts and section breaks.

## 4. Content Accuracy

- Preserve all original main-body text without omissions.
- Do not summarize, paraphrase, rewrite, or correct the original content.
- Preserve spelling, punctuation, terminology, and references.
- Maintain the original order of sections, paragraphs, tables, and figures.
- Preserve citations and bibliographic references exactly.
- Do not invent missing content.
- Pay particular attention to mathematical notation, symbols, and technical terminology.

## 5. Scanned Documents and OCR

If the PDF contains scanned pages or image-based text:

- Apply OCR to extract the text.
- Verify the extracted text against the original PDF.
- Distinguish main document content from excluded marginal notes.
- Preserve the original reading order.
- Reconstruct tables and other structured elements where possible.
- Avoid OCR-related character substitutions and missing text.

## 6. Quality Assurance

After conversion, compare the generated Word document with the original PDF.

Check that:

1. All main-content pages have been converted.
2. Headers and footers have been excluded.
3. Left-side notes and annotations have been excluded.
4. No main-body text has been accidentally removed.
5. Tables, figures, equations, and references are preserved.
6. Formatting and page layout are consistent.
7. The resulting document is fully editable in Microsoft Word.

Fix any identified issues before delivering the final document.

## 7. Output Requirements

- Generate a Microsoft Word document in `.docx` format.
- Use a meaningful filename based on the original PDF filename.
- Provide the completed DOCX file for download.
- Do not provide only extracted text or a conversion script.
- Include a short conversion report identifying any elements that could not be reconstructed accurately.

## 8. Final Instructions

Prioritize content accuracy and document readability.

Do not reproduce excluded headers, footers, or left-side notes, even if they appear on every page.

Do not make unnecessary changes to the original main content.

Preserve the original document's language.

Begin by inspecting the entire PDF, then perform the conversion and quality checks before delivering the final DOCX file.

Privacy requirement: The uploaded paper is confidential. Treat it as private research material. Do not retain, reuse, or disclose its contents beyond this conversation, and do not use it for model training.
